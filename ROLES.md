## The Data Lead (Pipeline & Engineering)

**Focus:** Responsible for accessing the data as soon as it is released on Friday at 5:00 PM.

**Tasks:** Setting up the database/CSV connections and ensuring the team has a clean "master" version of the data to explore.

**Why:** If the data isn't accessible or cleaned early, the rest of the team can't begin their individual work Saturday morning.

## The Algorithmic Architect (Efficiency & Big O)

**Focus:** Monitoring code performance to avoid $O(n^2)$ traps and ensuring scripts run efficiently on large datasets.

**Tasks:** Reviewing code in Pull Requests to ensure loops are optimized and memory management is sound.

**Why:** DataFest datasets are often massive; inefficient code will crash the environment during the final Sunday push.

## The Quality Assurance (QA) 

**Focus:** Validating that the data exploration results are accurate before they go into the final presentation.

**Tasks:** Checking for null values, logical errors in the analysis, and ensuring the "Clean Data" schema is being followed.

**Why:** Judges will look for accuracy; having one person dedicated to checking work prevents "bad data" from ruining the team's insights.

## The Communications & Presentation 

**Focus:** Managing the 5-minute maximum video and the 3-slide deck (1 cover + 2 content).

**Tasks:** Organizing the Saturday/Sunday 10:00 AM syncs and planning the meetings with the faculty advisor.

**Why:** Since CC students have "busy lives" and "other responsibilities," this role ensures everyone stays aligned with the submission requirements while working around their schedules.
