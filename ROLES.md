# Team 404 | DataFest 2026 – Core Leadership & Execution Roles

We keep leadership lean and collaborative in our small team. Two clear leadership roles + strong individual contributors.

## 1. L.O.G.I.C. Lead Architect
**Focus**: Overall vision, high-level system design, strategic technical direction, and architectural oversight.

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Defines the big-picture architecture, tech stack, data flow, and major design patterns
- Makes high-level decisions on trade-offs, scalability, modularity, and tool choices
- Ensures the solution aligns with DataFest goals, judging criteria, and time constraints
- Provides guidance on complex architectural questions and integration strategy
- Approves major design changes and resolves high-level conflicts

**Why this role is critical**:
The Lead Architect creates the blueprint that everything else builds on. A solid design prevents rework, enables parallel progress, and makes the final product more coherent and impressive.

**Assigned**: Curtis

## 2. Team Leader
**Focus**: Tactical execution, team coordination, daily progress, and getting things done.

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Translates architectural vision into actionable tasks, priorities, and sprint-like plans
- Assigns work, tracks progress, and removes blockers for the team
- Runs daily check-ins, stand-ups, or quick syncs (especially during crunch time)
- Facilitates code reviews, enforces basic standards, and helps resolve mid-level issues
- Keeps everyone aligned on deadlines, milestones, and the current focus
- Reports status/updates to the Lead Architect and flags risks early

**Why this role is critical**:
In a fast-moving competition like DataFest, someone needs to own the "how are we actually doing this today?" side. The Team Leader keeps momentum, prevents chaos, and makes sure we hit every checkpoint without burning out.

**Assigned**: Rodney
> "Don't stress it I'll help you with it" - Curtis

## 3. Senior Software Engineer + Developers (Team Contributors)
**Focus**: High-quality implementation, complex feature delivery, and collaborative problem-solving.

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Own major components (data pipelines, modeling, visualizations, analysis scripts, etc.)
- Write clean, efficient, documented, and tested code
- Solve difficult technical challenges and optimize critical parts
- Collaborate on prototyping, experimentation, and creative solutions
- Support each other through pair programming, explanations, and feedback
  
> Just Write Senior for lead, others are developers.

**Why this role is critical**:
These engineers bring the vision and plans to life. Their skills directly determine the depth, accuracy, polish, and innovation in our submission — key to standing out.
