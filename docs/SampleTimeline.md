# 🗓️ Sample DataFest for TYC Weekend Timeline

⚠️ **PM NOTE & TEAM**  
This timeline is provided as a **template idea** for how to approach the weekend.  
This is **not** our final set-in-stone plan, but rather a reference for official milestones and the required submission format.

## Friday at 5:00 pm (ET)

- Watch the introductory video(s) and read the important overview
- Access the data
- Teams either explore together or individually

## Saturday

- **8:00–10:00 am**  
  Work individually to explore the challenge material

- **10:00 am**  
  Meet together to brainstorm the team's approach  
  (devise a communication platform such as Teams, Discord, etc.)

- **10:00 am – 12:00 pm (noon)**  
  Work individually or together

- **Afternoon**  
  Plan meeting with faculty/advisor (Saturday afternoon)

- **Evening**  
  Continue to work individually

## Sunday

- **10:00 am**  
  Meet to discuss team progress

- **10:00 am – 12:00 pm (noon)**  
  Continue to work on data exploration

- **Afternoon**  
  Plan meeting with faculty/advisor (Sunday afternoon)

- **Afternoon**  
  Begin working on presentation  
  • 1 cover slide + 2 content slides  
  • 5-minute maximum video presentation

- **5:00 pm (ET)**  
  **Submit final presentation** (slides + video)

## Judging & Notes

- **Judging**  
  Judges will access and judge the submissions after the deadline

- **Awards**  
  Awards will be presented the following week

- **Flexibility**  
  We recognize that community college students have busy lives.  
  Some have jobs and other responsibilities, so teams may expect individuals to work around these commitments throughout the weekend.

---

I hope this helps, but you can always reach out to the organizers to ask additional questions.
